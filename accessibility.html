Accessibility

We want the StikStore website to be usable by as many people as possible. We use WCAG 2.2 Level AA as a development target.

Using this site

  • Navigate with a keyboard using Tab and Shift+Tab. A “Skip to content” link appears when focused.
  • Use Enter to follow links, and Enter or Space to activate buttons. Press Escape to close the mobile navigation.
  • Use your browser’s zoom and text-size settings. The layout adapts to smaller screens.
  • Choose a light or dark theme with the “Dark theme” button. Pressed means the dark theme is on.
  • The site respects your device’s reduced-motion preference and includes headings, landmarks, and text alternatives for screen readers.

Report a problem or request help

Email StephenDev0@outlook.com if something is difficult to access or you need the information in another format.

Include the page URL, what you were trying to do, and what went wrong. You can also share your browser or assistive technology if helpful. You do not need to share medical information.

Ongoing improvements

Accessibility work is ongoing, and some issues may remain. This statement covers the StikStore website. The app is still in development, and external sites such as X have their own accessibility features.
